# Research Question

Can institutional transitions be represented as first-class computational objects in a way that is deterministic, recoverable, replayable, auditable and semantically faithful to the authority and rules governing the transition?

Secondary questions concern conventional state machines, event sourcing, workflow systems, smart contracts, authority, rule-version changes, replay and recovery.

The research must permit the hypothesis to fail. If an existing execution model satisfies the canonical semantic vector without an abstraction substantively equivalent to IVM, the relevant novelty claim is weakened.
