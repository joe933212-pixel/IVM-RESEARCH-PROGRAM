# Research Archive Index

This document is the master index of the Institutional Virtual Machine research corpus.

The corpus includes conceptual research, institutional architecture, the reference runtime, runtime development history, experiments 001–005, prior-art and literature analysis, conformance work, comparative implementations, results and funding materials.

Experiments 001–003 established initial reference-runtime behaviour. Experiment 004 introduced failure-oriented testing and the process-death recovery question. Reference Runtime v0.4 represents the process-death recovery milestone. Experiment 005 begins comparative execution.

The prior-art analysis currently reaches **Section 53 — The Consequence for the IVM Architecture**. The continuation begins at **Section 54**.

Failed experiments are retained as research evidence wherever practical.

The programme does not assume its proposed abstractions are novel. The repository exists to make that question testable.
