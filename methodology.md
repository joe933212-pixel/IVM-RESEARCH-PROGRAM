# Research Methodology

A canonical institutional scenario is defined independently of implementation. Multiple execution models implement or represent the same scenario and are evaluated against a common semantic result vector.

The initial scenario is a business-licence application transitioning from PENDING to ACTIVE.

The experiment records event identity, authority, rule-set identity and version, pre-state, post-state, transition identity, commitment and history.

Additional tests introduce duplicate delivery, rule-version change, authority revocation, process death and competing transitions.

A comparator passes when it produces the required semantic result. Negative results remain part of the research record.
